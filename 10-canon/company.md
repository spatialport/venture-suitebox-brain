## Proposed knowledge (from workspace sources)

**What Suitebox is.** A Spatial Port internal venture (venture building): a **smart
vending machine di design for short-term rentals** — app-unlockable drawers, wireless
charging, remote management — that turns every apartment into a point of sale for
local typical products and travel essentials. Double value: better guest experience
+ a new recurring income stream for the host. Payoff: *"Il nuovo standard di
accoglienza."*

**Spatial Port's role** (Investor Update, June 2026): a 0→1 venture Spatial Port
helped build from the ground up — product concept and industrial design, brand,
host portal and app, photorealistic 3D, go-to-market; commercialization/marketing
phase was opening as of June 2026.

**Venture structure (draft, from term sheet).** 4 partners: 3 individuals + 1
company ("ExistingCo", the founder's company, which holds IP/trademark and puts in
cash). Plan: constitute a **NewCo SRL "startup innovativa"**, transfer IP as
individual assets (not a business branch), work-for-equity for the three operating
partners (Marketing / Production-Logistics-Import / Software), draft role-equity
split 34/22/22/22 with ExistingCo rising to ~45–55% after cash + IP. Vesting 4y /
12m cliff. Status of signature/constitution: unknown.
