# Offer

Purpose: Products/services, economics, proof, differentiation and commercial priorities.

## Accepted knowledge

None accepted yet. Proposed below (numbers from the June 2026 pricing doc, derived

## Proposed knowledge

**Commercial model.** Host buys hardware (one-off) + annual service fee + **5%
commission** on guest transactions (collected centrally in app). A rental option
(activation fee + monthly subscription + commission) exists for the Swiss/luxury
market, shown only as "soluzioni su misura".

**Hardware line (prices ex-VAT; CH in CHF +30–40%):**

| Configuration | Hardware | Service/yr |
|---|---:|---:|
| SUITEBOX da Tavolo (single module) | €190 | €80 |
| SUITEBOX Parete (double module, wall) | €490 | €150 |
| SUITEBOX Verticale (floor, coat rack) | €760 | €150 |

Volume discounts: −10% (6–25), −15% (26–100), up to −20% (>100, negotiated).
Shipping €15/unit.

**Host economics (base scenario, 65% occupancy).** Payback ~4.9 months (Tavolo),
~11.1 (Parete), ~13.7 (Verticale); host margin on products 65–70%. Revenue share
for cleaning companies/gestori: 30% (€57–254/box/yr across scenarios).

**Assortment.** 60–70% food / 30–40% no-food, seasonal rotation, regional
localization. Product partners identified: Fiore di Puglia, Borghi d'Italia,
Mionetto, Bottega, Allegrini Amenities, Camille Bloch, Frey.

**Service includes.** Single European guest account · pay-per-unlock · wireless
charging · host portal with recommended assortment and dropshipping auto-reorder ·
real-time stock telemetry · monthly statement and payout · support and updates.

**Investor offer (June 2026 one-pager).** Round €1M SAFE/convertible, cap €4.5M,
20% discount (~18% dilution at cap); alternatives €800k/€1.2M. Projections (base):
1,000 boxes yr1 → 10,606 yr3; revenue €316,542 → €3,547,022; EBITDA positive from
yr2, ~44% margin yr3. Additional streams: supplier portal fee (12%), brand
placement (yr2+).
