# Brand Book

Foundation for generated content. Do not scale content production until Alex
accepts it. Source of truth: `SUITEBOX_Brand.md` (MIILOO, giugno 2026, v1 +
Daylight v2 update) and `SUITEBOX_Brandbook.html`.

## Brand truth

- Purpose: bring hotel-grade hospitality (minibar/room service) to short-term rentals.
- Belief: an in-room product of design beats delivery; hospitality is a revenue stream, not a cost.
- Promise: to the host — extra income and premium perception, effortless; to the guest — everything needed, within reach of the room.
- Reasons to believe: payback 5–14 months, host margin 65–70%, 3 PM preorders, named product partners, remote management.
- Five-year direction: Italy → CH → FR → ES/DACH; "il nuovo standard di accoglienza" as category default.

## Audience

- Primary ICP: property managers; then single hosts (1–3 units) and cleaning companies.
- Context and job: raise revenue per unit and reviews without adding work.
- Emotional stakes: "my apartment feels like a boutique hotel".
- Objections: workload, thin margins, fiscal complexity of micro-sales, "will it pay back?".
- Language customers actually use: to be collected from demos/preorders (open).

## Positioning and message

- Category: smart vending machine di design per affitti brevi.
- Unique advantage: design furniture + local typical products + low-risk model + auto-replenishing supplier portal.
- Alternatives: status quo, hotel minibars, US micro-vending, delivery.
- Core message: double value host+guest; payoff *Il nuovo standard di accoglienza.*
- Supporting messages: "Trasforma ogni alloggio in un punto vendita." · "Reddito extra, gestito da remoto." · "Tutto ciò che serve, a portata di stanza."
- Prohibited/unproven claims: superlatives, invented testimonials, unverified stats.

## Voice

- Sounds like: clear, direct, concrete, hospitable but professional; short sentences.
- Never sounds like: saccharine, hypey, jargon-heavy.
- Vocabulary to use: standard, accoglienza, design, reddito, da remoto, locale, essenziale.
- Vocabulary to avoid: "rivoluzionario", "il migliore", empty tech jargon.
- Bilingual-ready: Italian primary, EN replicable.

## Visual and creative taste

- Desired feeling: "tech-luxury di prossimità" — digital precision + Italian warmth; elegant, essential, technological, hospitable.
- Color/type/image principles: **Daylight system (v2)** — paper `#F6F4EF`, ink `#14161A`, blue accent `#2D6BFF` (single action accent), green/amber/coral functionals; navy/gold palette **deprecated**. Type: Unbounded (700/300/500, display only) + Inter (text/UI). Logo: SUITE bold + BOX light, uppercase, monochrome, weights never inverted; SB monogram.
- Photography: real short-let interiors, natural warm light, the box naturally in the furniture; macro details (drawers, wireless charging, app). No fake renders.
- Layout: generous whitespace, clean grids, hairlines, near-sharp corners (2–8 px radius); big Unbounded numbers with small Inter labels.
- Bad references: industrial vending aesthetics; anything "macchinetta".
- Human taste reviewer: Alex.

## Channel adaptation

Constant: Daylight palette, logo rules, tone, double-value message. Varies: site
(commercial, two entrances host/guest), Data Room (investor sobriety), partner kit
(sales pragmatism), social (guest experience storytelling). Known misalignment: the
pitch deck still uses Georgia serif — to be brought onto the system.
