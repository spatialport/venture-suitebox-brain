## Proposed knowledge

**Category owned.** *Smart vending machine di design per affitti brevi* — a nascent
category; no direct competitor in Italy (first-mover claim, June 2026).

**One-liner.** Suitebox turns every short-let into a point of sale for local
products and travel essentials, improving the guest stay and creating new host
income — all managed remotely. Payoff: *Il nuovo standard di accoglienza.*

**Core strategy: the double value.** Never pick one side — host (extra income,
premium perception, zero effort) AND guest (everything needed, in the room). Every
page must land both in under 10 seconds. The host is the buyer; the guest is the
beneficiary who justifies the purchase.

**Alternatives and why Suitebox wins.** Status quo (no monetization) → h24 in-room
service + recurring income · hotel minibars (Bartech, Minibar Systems) → design
furniture, autonomous install, low-risk model · US micro-vending (Qvie, EasyBar) →
premium aesthetics, local typical products, supplier portal · delivery services →
an always-present product, not an on-demand courier.

**Four moats.** Design furniture · local typical products focus · low-risk model
for the host · supplier portal with automatic replenishment (lock-in).

**Messaging pillars.** 1) Design that elevates the property. 2) Income that pays
itself back (transparent ROI). 3) Zero effort, all remote. 4) Premium guest
experience.

**Proof points (pre-launch honesty rule).** 3 PM preorders (30 units) · Swiss
exclusive partner active · named product partners · market data from 27 sources.
No customer reviews or installed base yet — never invent testimonials.

**Prohibited claims.** Gratuitous superlatives ("rivoluzionario", "il migliore"),
useless technical jargon, unverified numeric claims.
