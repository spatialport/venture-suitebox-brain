## Proposed knowledge

**Buyers (B2B, primary targets):**

1. **Property manager (PRIMARY).** 10–300+ units. Wants revenue per unit without
   extra work; one contract for the fleet; volume pricing; no technicians for
   install. Objections: team workload, thin margins, micro-sales fiscal complexity.
   Proof that moves them: the 3 existing preorders of 10 units each.
2. **Single host/owner (1–3 units).** Design- and review-sensitive; more emotional
   purchase. Needs simple buying, DIY install, certainty it pays back (ROI
   calculator; payback 5–14 months).
3. **Cleaning company / operating manager.** Already in the apartment at every
   turnover; converts a cost center into revenue via ~30% revenue share.

**Eligibility constraint.** Hosts must have a **P.IVA** (or be cleaning companies);
private individuals are excluded (cannot handle VAT). This deliberately narrows the
market and must be verified at onboarding.

**Beneficiary (not buyer).** The guest/traveler: forgot toothbrush 22% / charger
19% / sunscreen 18%; 52% of travelers in Italy buy local typical products; average
€53 spent replacing forgotten items. The guest story convinces the host (reviews).

**Influencer.** Investor — separate path (Data Room), not the commercial homepage.

**Geography.** Launch Italy (~508,000 active listings, 621,262 CIN registered) +
Switzerland (~51,000; exclusive partner active). Expansion plan: France (yr2),
Spain + DACH (yr3). ~25% of listings professionally managed → multi-unit orders.
