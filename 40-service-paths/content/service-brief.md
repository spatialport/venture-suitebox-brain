# Content Service Brief

## Business objective

Convert hosts/PMs through the site and sales materials; equip procacciatori and
partners; support the investor track.

## Audience and context

Two declared entrances from the hero: "host/gestori" (sell) and "esperienza ospite"
(story). Every page must convey the double value (host income + guest service) in
under 10 seconds.

## Accepted constraints

- Claims must be verifiable from the business plan (payback ~5–14 months, host margin
  65–70%, 3 preorders × 10 units); no invented testimonials in pre-launch.
- Prohibited words per brand: "rivoluzionario", "il migliore", empty tech jargon.

## Deliverables

Production site (14 pages incl. ROI calculator, checkout, partner area) · copy deck ·
site IA · partner/procacciatori kit (landing pages, scripts, email templates, one-pager) ·
sales kit · social content plan · ad creative · investor one-pager/memo/pitch contents.

## Definition of done

Live pages match the copy deck and Daylight system; partner kit usable by a new
procacciatore without assistance; numeric claims validated against the current BP model.
