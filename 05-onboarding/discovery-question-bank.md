# Discovery Question Bank (venture)

Capture answers as evidence first. Promote only durable, consequential knowledge.
For a paused internal venture, the highest-value questions concern reactivation.

## Venture status and reactivation

- Why exactly is the venture paused (capital, team bandwidth, fiscal setup, partner alignment)?
- What single event would trigger reactivation (funding, NewCo constituted, pilot host signed)?
- Is the mid-September go-live plan (`SUITEBOX_Piano_Operativo_MetaSettembre.md`) still the plan, or superseded?
- What is the state of the 400 Boxotto units and the Varedo stock today?

## Company and structure

- Is ExistingCo (holder of IP/cash per the term sheet) Spatial Port itself, or a third company?
- Has the interim partner agreement / work-for-equity been signed? Cap table state?
- Has the NewCo SRL "startup innovativa" been constituted? Trademark registered to whom?

## Customer and market

- Status of the 3 property-manager preorders (30 units) — still warm?
- Status of the Swiss exclusive partner and the Fratelli Desideri partnership?
- Which pilot host(s) are available for the first end-to-end apartment test?

## Offer and economics

- Are the published prices (€190/€490/€760 + canone + 5%) still current?
- Investor round (€1M SAFE, cap €4.5M): open, closed, or shelved?

## Operations

- Who is the interim GM and the single technical owner (box + software)?
- Assembly capacity (pieces/week) with unlock electronics included?
- Stripe Connect setup state (host = merchant of record, application_fee)?

## Brand and taste

- Who has final taste authority for site, social, product photography (default: Alex)?
